Sleep behaviour

Duration is the timer the device sleeps on, and is what "Wake and redraw" in the editor sets. It also picks the sleep mode, which is why there is no control for one: under 5 minutes the device light-sleeps, because waking in place beats paying for a boot; from 5 minutes up it deep-sleeps. The retry window is how long "Push to display" keeps resending the image (every 45s) waiting for the device to confirm the write; sleep is only sent once it does.
